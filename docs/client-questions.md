# Questions for Beyond Limits

The new Get Started page asks families one or two questions and then shows only the forms they need. The form rules below are our best guess from the current site. Please confirm or correct each one.

## Who fills out which forms

1. **General Admissions Form.** Is this the main form for every new speech, OT and PT patient?
2. **Upper Extremity Intake.** Who uses the "General Admission with Upper Extremity Intake" form? We assumed OT patients whose main concern is arm or hand use. Does PT use it too?
3. **Feeding.** Does a feeding therapy patient fill out the ST/OT Feeding Intake *and* the General Admissions Form, or only the feeding form?
4. **Audiology.** The audiology forms cover newborn to 12 months and 13 months to school age. What does a school-age child or teen (up to 21) coming for a hearing test fill out?
5. **Payment Consent.** Does every new family sign this?
6. **Telehealth.** Is telehealth still offered? If so, for which services?
7. **Patient Update Form.** When should current families use it: once a year, or only when something changes?
8. **Multiple therapies.** Does a child starting two therapies (say speech and OT) need one General Admissions Form or one per service?
9. **Veterans (VES).** Do VES patients need any of your forms, or does VES send everything?

## Getting forms back

10. What's the preferred way to return forms: email, fax, bring them in, or an online form? Would they like fillable online forms down the road?
11. Is email OK for completed forms, given they contain health information? (If not, we'll steer families to fax or in person.)
12. Who should the website contact form go to, and who answers it?

## Facts to confirm

13. **Hours.** The live site says Monday to Friday, 8 to 6. The developer's build says Monday to Thursday. Which is right?
14. **Insurance.** Is the in-network list still current (Amerigroup, CareSource, Peach State, BlueCross, Ambetter, Tricare, and Wellcare for audiology only)?
15. **Wait times.** Is "about two weeks" from evaluation to first therapy visit still accurate?
16. **Tours.** Do they offer clinic tours, and how should families book one?

## Careers

17. Which roles are they hiring for now?
18. What makes the team a great place to work: mentorship, CEU support, schedule flexibility, benefits? We'd like real details, not generic claims.
19. Can we get team photos and short "why I love working here" quotes from two or three therapists?
20. How should people apply: email, an online form, or a job board link?

## Photos and brand

21. Can we get the logo files (SVG or high-res PNG) and their brand colors?
22. Do they have, or can we take, real photos of the clinic, gyms and staff? Stock photos are a weaker draw for recruiting.
23. Do the provider profiles need sign-off from each provider before launch?
